Brown pelican plumage, face, upper bill and gular pouch textures are adapted
from "Brown Pelican", Lee Karney / U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, 2015.
The source is listed as public domain.
https://www.fws.gov/media/brown-pelican-7

This asset contains cropped and reprojected photographic details, combined
with original procedural surface textures for clothing, feet and bicycle.
